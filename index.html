About Me

Crafting digital experiences for the gaming community since 2020

Pakistan

Hey there! I'm Arbaz Khan, a passionate Discord banner designer hailing from the vibrant streets of Pakistan. With over 5 years of experience in the digital design space, I've specialized in creating eye-catching banners that make gaming communities stand out on Discord.

My journey started in 2019, designing banners for local gaming clans in Karachi and Lahore. After gaining recognition within the Pakistani gaming scene, I expanded my reach globally, now serving communities across North America, Europe, and Asia. I understand that a great Discord banner is more than just art — it's your community's identity.
